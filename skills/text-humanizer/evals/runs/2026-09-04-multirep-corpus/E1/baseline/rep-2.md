Here's what's new in Vantage 3.2.

This release rethinks how teams work with their data, aiming for something faster and more straightforward to use day to day.

In our internal benchmark, query latency dropped from 840 ms to 210 ms. The new export pipeline handles 2.4 million rows without paging. SSO now supports Okta and Azure AD.

We built this update with a lot of care, and we think it shows.
