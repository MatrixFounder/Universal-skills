# Meridian rollout: where the programme stands

The pilot is done. The number that decides the business case isn't in yet.

Meridian isn't a scanner upgrade. It rewrites how a shift closes. At Kelvin Freight, crews push 9,400 scans through the handhelds on a busy day, and shift close is down from 22 minutes. How far down for September, we can't say yet: the audit logs still need reconciling, and we're not printing a guess in its place.

The second site, Tilburg, comes online once racking is finished. Deb Oyelaran's crew signed off on the integration test on 17 October.

Thursday's session goes through the numbers in full, September figure included once it's in.
