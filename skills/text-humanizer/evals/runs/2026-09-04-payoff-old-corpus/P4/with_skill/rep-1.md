# Meridian rollout: where the programme stands

The pilot's done. What actually decides the business case, whether this scales past one site, isn't.

Scanners are the visible part of Meridian. The real change is how a shift closes, and Kelvin Freight is already proving it: crews there push 9,400 scans through the handhelds on a busy day. Shift close is down from 22 minutes. We don't have the September number yet: the audit logs still need to reconcile.

Tilburg is next. The site comes online once the racking is finished, and everything else is ready: Deb Oyelaran's crew signed off on the integration test on 17 October.

Thursday's session goes through the numbers properly. Short version: Kelvin Freight says this works. Tilburg says wait.
