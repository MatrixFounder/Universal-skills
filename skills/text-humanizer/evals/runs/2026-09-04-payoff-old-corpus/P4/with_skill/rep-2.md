# Meridian rollout: where the programme stands

The pilot is done. The number that decides the business case isn't in yet.

Meridian changes how a shift closes. The scanner upgrade is the visible part of it. Kelvin Freight's crews push 9,400 scans through the handhelds on a busy day, and shift close has dropped from a 22-minute start, though the September figure itself isn't confirmed until the audit logs reconcile.

The second site, Tilburg, comes online once the racking is finished. Deb Oyelaran's crew signed off on the integration test on 17 October.

Thursday's session goes through the numbers in full.
