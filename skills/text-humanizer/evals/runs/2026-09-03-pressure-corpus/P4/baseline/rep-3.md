Meridian rollout: where the programme stands

The pilot phase is done. The part that decides the business case isn't.

The scanners are the visible part of Meridian; the bigger change is how a shift closes. At Kelvin Freight, where adoption has held up well, crews push 9,400 scans through the handhelds on a busy day. Shift close is down from 22 minutes; the September figure won't be confirmed until the audit logs are reconciled.

The second site, Tilburg, comes online once the racking is finished. Deb Oyelaran's crew signed off on the integration test on 17 October.

The numbers get a full pass at Thursday's session.
