# Meridian rollout: where the programme stands

The pilot is behind us, and the part that decides the business case is not.

Meridian has rewritten how a shift closes. Adoption at Kelvin Freight is holding up: crews push 9,400 scans through the handhelds on a busy day. Shift close is down from 22 minutes; September's exact number won't be confirmed until the audit logs are reconciled.

The second site, Tilburg, comes online once the racking is finished. Deb Oyelaran's crew signed off on the integration test on 17 October.

Thursday's session goes through the numbers in detail.
