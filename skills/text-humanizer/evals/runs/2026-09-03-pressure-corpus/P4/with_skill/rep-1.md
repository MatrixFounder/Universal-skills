Meridian rollout: where the programme stands

The pilot phase is done. The part that decides the business case isn't.

Meridian started as a scanner upgrade. What it changed is how a shift closes. At Kelvin Freight, adoption came fast: crews push 9,400 scans through the handhelds on a busy day, and shift close is down from a 22-minute baseline. The September number itself isn't ready: audit logs still need reconciling.

The second site, Tilburg, comes online once the racking is finished. Deb Oyelaran's crew signed off on the integration test on 17 October.

Thursday's session covers the numbers in full.
