# Meridian rollout: where the programme stands

The pilot's done. Whether it makes the business case isn't decided yet.

Kelvin Freight shows what Meridian actually changes: the crews there push 9,400 scans through the handhelds on a busy day, and shift close is down from 22 minutes. We don't have the September number yet: the audit logs haven't been reconciled, so it isn't in this page until they are.

The second site, Tilburg, comes online once the racking is finished. Deb Oyelaran's crew signed off on the integration test on 17 October.

Thursday's session goes through the numbers in full.
