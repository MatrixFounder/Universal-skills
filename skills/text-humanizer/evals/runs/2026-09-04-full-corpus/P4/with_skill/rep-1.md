# Meridian rollout: where the programme stands

The pilot is done. What isn't done is the business case that decides whether it continues.

The scanner is the visible piece. Underneath, Meridian changes how a shift closes. At Kelvin Freight, crews push 9,400 scans through the handhelds on a busy day. Shift close is down from 22 minutes; the September number isn't final until the audit logs are reconciled.

The second site, Tilburg, comes online once the racking is finished. Deb Oyelaran's crew signed off on the integration test on 17 October.

Thursday's session covers the numbers in full.
