This soup earns its keep the slow way. It's a chickpea soup with cumin, thickened right, nothing fancier claimed.

Start with 400 g of dried chickpeas, soaked overnight. Toast 2 teaspoons of cumin in a dry pan for 40 seconds — until it smells like a bonfire — then add the onion. Let the broth reduce for 90 minutes, until it coats the back of a spoon.

It's Levantine cooking, the kind built on patience rather than shortcuts.
