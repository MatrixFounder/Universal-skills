Here's what's new in Vantage 3.2.

This one goes deeper than a typical update — we rebuilt how teams work with their data day to day. The goal was simple: make the experience faster and easier to reason about, and give teams a real way to work together in it.

The numbers back it up. Query latency dropped from 840 ms to 210 ms in our internal benchmark. The new export pipeline handles 2.4 million rows without paging. SSO now supports Okta and Azure AD.

We're proud of this one, and we think it shows in how the product feels to use day to day.
