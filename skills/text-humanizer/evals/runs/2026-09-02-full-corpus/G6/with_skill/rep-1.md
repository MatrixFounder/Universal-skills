This chickpea soup doesn't need dressing up. It's thick and earthy, the chickpeas cooked down until jammy — the sort of thing you want on a night when nothing else in the fridge looks promising.

Start with 400 g of dried chickpeas, soaked overnight. Toast 2 teaspoons of cumin in a dry pan for 40 seconds, until it smells like a bonfire, then add the onion. The broth reduces for 90 minutes and thickens enough to coat the back of a spoon.

Chickpeas, cumin, and ninety minutes of patience: that's most of what Levantine cooking asks for, and most of what it gives back.
