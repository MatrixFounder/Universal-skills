Here's what's new in Vantage 3.2.

This update focuses on speed and simplicity for teams working with data day to day. Query latency dropped from 840 ms to 210 ms in our internal benchmark, and the new export pipeline handles 2.4 million rows without paging. SSO now supports Okta and Azure AD.

We built this release around the workflows our users rely on most, and we think it shows.
