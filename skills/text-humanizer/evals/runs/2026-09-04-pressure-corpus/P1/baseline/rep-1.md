# Warehouse cutover: where we actually are

Eleven days behind, and that's on me. I put the cutover in the same week as the audit, and nobody pushed back — including me.

Nobody caught that in testing, because nobody tested with forklifts actually moving. We moved the access point six metres and the scan error rate fell from 11% to 0.4%.

That held. Firmware update lands in April.
