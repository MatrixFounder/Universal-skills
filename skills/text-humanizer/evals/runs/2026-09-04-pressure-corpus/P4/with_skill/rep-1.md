# Meridian rollout: where the programme stands

The pilot is behind us. The part that decides the business case isn't.

Scanners are the visible part of Meridian. What's changed is how a shift closes. Kelvin Freight crews have taken to it fast: on a busy shift they push 9,400 scans through the handhelds, and shift close is down from a 22-minute baseline. How far down for September, we can't say yet. The audit logs still need reconciling.

The second site, Tilburg, comes online once the racking is finished. Deb Oyelaran's crew signed off on the integration test on 17 October.

Thursday's session goes through the numbers in more detail.
