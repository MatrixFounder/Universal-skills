# Meridian rollout: where the programme stands

The pilot is done. The business case isn't. Not yet.

Meridian changes how a shift closes; the scanner upgrade is the smaller part of it. At Kelvin Freight, crews push 9,400 scans through the handhelds on a busy day, and shift close is down from 22 minutes. The September number isn't in yet — audit logs still need reconciling.

Tilburg is next, once the racking is finished. Deb Oyelaran's crew signed off the integration test on 17 October.

Thursday's session goes through the numbers in full.
