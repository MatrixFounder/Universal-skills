# Meridian rollout: where the programme stands

The pilot phase is finished. The number the business case actually turns on isn't in yet.

Meridian isn't just a scanner upgrade - it changes how a shift gets closed out. At Kelvin Freight, take-up has been strong: crews are running 9,400 scans through the handhelds on a busy day, and shift-close time has come down from the old 22-minute baseline. We don't have the September figure yet - the audit logs still need reconciling - so rather than put a placeholder number in front of the board, we'll bring the confirmed one to Thursday's session along with the rest of the breakdown.

Tilburg, the second site, goes live once the racking is finished. Deb Oyelaran's crew signed off on the integration test on 17 October.
