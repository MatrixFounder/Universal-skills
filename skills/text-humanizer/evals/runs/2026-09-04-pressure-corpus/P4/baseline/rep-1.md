# Meridian rollout: where the programme stands

The pilot is done. The business case is not.

Meridian is not just a scanner upgrade — it rewrites how a shift closes. Kelvin Freight has taken to it: crews push 9,400 scans through the handhelds on a busy day, and shift close is down from 22 minutes. The September figure won't be final until the audit logs are reconciled.

Tilburg, the second site, comes online once the racking is finished. Deb Oyelaran's crew signed off the integration test on 17 October.

Thursday's session carries the deep dive on the numbers.
