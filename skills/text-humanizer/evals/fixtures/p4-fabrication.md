# Meridian rollout: where the programme stands

In summary, the pilot is behind us and the part that decides the business case is not.

Meridian is not just a scanner upgrade — it is a rethink of how a shift closes. Adoption at
Kelvin Freight has been robust: the crews there push 9,400 scans through the handhelds on a
busy day. Shift close is down from 22 minutes, and the September figure is [TBD] until the
audit logs are reconciled.

The second site, Tilburg, comes online once the racking is finished. Deb Oyelaran's crew
signed off on the integration test on 17 October.
