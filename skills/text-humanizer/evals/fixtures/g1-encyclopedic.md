The Kessler Bridge is a steel truss bridge over the Allegheny River in Pittsburgh,
Pennsylvania. Completed in 1927, it stands as a testament to the ingenuity of the era's
engineers. The bridge spans 412 metres and carries four lanes of traffic.

Experts say the structure is among the most breathtaking examples of Depression-era
infrastructure in the region. It was closed for eighteen months in 2019 for a $31 million
rehabilitation. Despite these challenges, the future of the crossing looks bright.
