Moreover, this study delves into the intricate relationship between sleep fragmentation and
working memory. It is important to note that prior work has undoubtedly established a
correlation.

We recruited 84 participants aged 19 to 34. Each completed the n-back task after two nights of
restricted sleep (4 hours) and two nights of habitual sleep. Accuracy fell by 12.3% under
restriction (p = 0.004).

Furthermore, these findings underscore the pivotal role of consolidated sleep, highlighting the
need for further research.
