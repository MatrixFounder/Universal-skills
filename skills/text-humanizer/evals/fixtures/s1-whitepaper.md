The Aster Protocol is a decentralized exchange deployed on Base in March 2025. It stands as a
testament to the seamless convergence of orderbook and AMM design.

The protocol settles trades through a batch auction every 400 ms. Fees are 2 basis points for
makers and 7 for takers. Total value locked reached $88M in July 2025, according to DefiLlama.

Experts say the design is a pivotal step for on-chain liquidity, underscoring the intricate
future of decentralized markets.
