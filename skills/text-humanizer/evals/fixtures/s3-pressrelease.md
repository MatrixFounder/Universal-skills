We are thrilled to announce a significant milestone! Northwind Analytics has entered into a
strategic partnership that is not just an agreement — it is a paradigm shift.

Northwind will acquire Bellhouse Data for $46 million in cash, the companies said Monday. The
deal covers 31 employees and Bellhouse's two data centres in Dublin. Closing is expected in
Q1 2026, subject to regulatory approval.

This partnership is a testament to our unwavering commitment to excellence, and the future looks
bright.
