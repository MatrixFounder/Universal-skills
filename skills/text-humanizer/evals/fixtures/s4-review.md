This restaurant is absolutely delicious and offers a truly vibrant, seamless, and intricate
dining experience. It is not just dinner — it is a journey.

We went to Sable on a Thursday in November. The bill for two was 84 pounds without wine. The
lamb neck had been cooked for six hours and pulled apart under a fork; the bread arrived twenty
minutes late and cold.

Undoubtedly, this restaurant serves as a testament to the tapestry of modern British cooking.
