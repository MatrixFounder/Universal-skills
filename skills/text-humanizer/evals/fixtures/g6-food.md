This soup is absolutely delicious and the flavors are simply mouth-watering. It is not just a
soup — it is a symphony of flavors.

Start with 400 g of dried chickpeas soaked overnight. Toast 2 teaspoons of cumin in the dry pan
for 40 seconds until it smells like a bonfire, then add the onion. The broth reduces for 90
minutes and thickens enough to coat the back of a spoon.

Undoubtedly, this dish serves as a testament to the vibrant tapestry of Levantine cooking.
